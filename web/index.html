Battery details

Remaining charge of the robot itself (rt/lf/bmsstate). Voltage is converted as mV and current as mA, so these are estimates (the units are unverified in the documentation). A negative current is treated as discharging.

Attitude (IMU)

rt/lowstate field imu_state. Angles are converted from rad to degrees.

Odometry

rt/odommodestate. The position is an estimate from the start position and drifts.

Jetson and main board

The Jetson values are read from /proc and /sys; the main board values come from rt/lf/mainboardstate. The meaning of the main board's value is unverified (the raw value is shown).

Remote controller, emergency stop and services

Event-type topics show "No data" if nothing has happened. "No data" does not mean something is wrong.

Camera video

lidar (point cloud)

Checking…

LiDAR unitChecking…

Stopped

Relays and shows the point cloud from lidar_stream.py on the Jetson (it only subscribes to rt/utlidar/cloud_livox_mid360 and never touches the motors). A single frame (0.1 s) is sparse by nature, so stacking the last few frames reveals the shape of walls and floors (best while the G1 stands still; it leaves trails while walking). The raw cloud is about 20k points (about 11k valid) at 10 Hz, and "Point density" selects the thinning on the Jetson side (coarse 0.4 MB/s, normal 0.9 MB/s, all points 1.8 MB/s). Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Color shows height (blue = low, red = high, relative across all stacked points). Coordinates stay in livox_frame (lidar origin, in m) and may be tilted relative to the G1's heading. Circles show distance (m); the red line is the x axis and the green line is the y axis. Fetching stops when you leave the tab.
